# Religion and history

Source: notes from Harlan's study session in the faith channel, Oct 28-29 2026. Figures are as noted there; verify before citing.

## Four families of traditions
- Abrahamic (Judaism, Christianity, Islam): revealed law.
- Dharmic (Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, Sikhism): karma, liberation.
- East Asian (Confucianism, Daoism, Shinto): harmony, virtue through roles.
- Philosophical (Stoicism, Aristotle, humanism): reason alone.
- Key divide: the source of moral authority.

## Judaism
- Covenant; 613 commandments; deed over creed.
- Hillel: "What is hateful to you, do not do to your neighbor. The rest is commentary."
- Tikkun olam.

## Faith vs works
- Paul (faith) vs Letter of James ("faith without works is dead").
- Islam pairs "believe and do righteous deeds."

## Reformation
- Luther's 95 Theses, Oct 31 1517, against indulgences; sola fide, sola scriptura; printing press.
- Calvin, Zwingli, Henry VIII; Council of Trent.
- Wars of religion to the Peace of Westphalia, 1648.
- 1999 Lutheran-Catholic Joint Declaration on the Doctrine of Justification.

## Christian branches (~2.4B)
- Catholic ~1.3B.
- Eastern Orthodox ~250M (Great Schism 1054).
- Oriental Orthodox (split 451).
- Protestant ~900M: Lutheran, Reformed, Anglican, Anabaptist, Methodist, Baptist, Pentecostal (1906).

## Ideas
- Chesterton's fence.
- Hirschman: exit, voice, loyalty.
- Weber: the Protestant work ethic.
- Heschel: the Sabbath as "a palace in time."

## US religion (Pew)
- 2014, by state: Protestant largest in 43 states; Catholic in NY, NJ, MA, RI; LDS in Utah; no religion in NH, VT.
- 2023-24: Christian 62%; Protestant 40% (evangelical 23, mainline 11, historically Black 5); Catholic 19%; unaffiliated 29%. United Methodist under 3%, down from 5% in 2007.
