CORE & CRAFT · Tabulae · the town, full screen · the pick

Duae Insulaetwo islands in isometric

The pick, Oct 29. The town as an isometric map that fills the board: C.O.R.E. and C.R.A.F.T. as two islands joined by a bridge, each pillar a tower that gains a storey per level, its Castra week laid in front as seven floor tiles. This page is the reference for the OWL build; spec.md beside it says how to build it.

It's drawn with Canvas shapes, not glyphs, so it looks more like a game and less like the typing tile next to it. The rail under the map stays in the mono font.

Switch the theme and the state for all three at once. "Fit" shrinks them to see together; at 100% they're real size and the row scrolls sideways. Below: a comparison, the Popups and rail they share, each map's own taps and build notes, and the synthetic data.

What "full screen" means here

The town tile stays the 6x3 Forum tile on the board. Tapping it runs screen(), and the map covers the whole board, 6 columns by 8 rows (616 by 824 px at the brief's grid), until ✕ runs close(). The other tiles can't show while it's open, so their essentials are tucked into a rail under the map: the route, level and streak, check-ins and the add box.

Rules kept from the tiles

  • Pillars read as the spec writes them, in English, under C.O.R.E. and C.R.A.F.T., with each acronym letter in the accent color.
  • Latin only in eyebrows, as "Latin · English". Roman numerals for levels only; xp, counts and days in digits.
  • Only the theme's named colors, the mono font, and OWL's nodes: one Canvas for the map, Rows, Columns, Texts, a Progress, a Sparkline, an Input, Buttons and Popups around it.
  • From Forum: nine buildings that grow with level, lit windows for today, @ at the top quest, ? for a check-in, night by the clock, rain for overdue quests.
  • From Castra: every building has its fenced block with the level numeral, the week (yellow today, green earlier in 7 days) and the 7-day count; the route's stops are numbered beside the roofs.

What git showed (main at 586fd1e, read only)

  • PR 8 added acta quest, acta checkin and acta rest, so every tap below is now a real verb with --via board, and undo finds the done event itself.
  • PR 9 put the game into snapshot.json: ranked quests with score and reasons, proposals, check-ins, xp, levels, streak, 30 days of history, and per-pillar level, xp and events_7d.
  • Board.qml already has the hook for this: a widget's screen { } covers the board until close(), drawn at 0.96 over the tiles with a 12 dp margin and up to 1,000 nodes. Its comment says "The town uses this."

Nodes

  • One Canvas for the map: text runs for Vicus, shapes for the other two (about 350 and 450 calls). Canvas calls aren't nodes, and the rail is about 60, so a screen stays far under OWL's 1,000.
  • Map taps use the Canvas's onTap(x, y); the concepts' build notes give the hit boxes.

Snapshot fields these still need

  • pillars[].today: events today per pillar, for lit windows and Castra's ▪. PR 9 has only events_7d.
  • leveled_today: pillars that levelled up today, for the yellow ✦.
  • clock comes from OWL's clock source, not the snapshot.

Build notes

  • Drawn with Canvas path(points, closed: true) for every face and tile, circle and line for roofs and rain, and text() for labels. No glyph art at all: this one leaves the ASCII house style.
  • Lots sit on an iso grid: x = ox + (i − j) × 56, y = oy + (i + j) × 28 (in px; ÷ 1.39 for dp). Draw lots in order of i + j so nearer towers cover farther ones. A tap maps back with the inverse and picks the lot whose diamond holds it.
  • Faces use named colors only: left muted, right muted in a layer(0.55), top surface. Night is the same drawing with stars and the moon; only windows and the @ glow.
