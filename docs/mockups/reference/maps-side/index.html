CORE & CRAFT · Tabulae · the town, full screen · three concepts

Three town maps

Forum with Castra, as a map that fills the board. Each concept keeps Forum's buildings and puts every one on its Castra block with its whole week, but clusters the nine pillars its own way and adds something of its own: a river, a hill, a walled wheel.

Switch the theme and the state for all three at once. "Fit three" shrinks them to see together; at 100% they're real size and the row scrolls sideways. Below: a comparison, the Popups and rail they share, each map's own taps and build notes, and the synthetic data.

What "full screen" means here

The town tile stays the 6x3 Forum tile on the board. Tapping it runs screen(), and the map covers the whole board, 6 columns by 8 rows (616 by 824 px at the brief's grid), until ✕ runs close(). The other tiles can't show while it's open, so their essentials are tucked into a rail under the map: the route, level and streak, check-ins and the add box.

If you meant the whole monitor, the board's columns setting would have to grow first, and these would be laid out wide.

Rules kept from the tiles

  • Pillars read as the spec writes them, in English, under C.O.R.E. and C.R.A.F.T., with each acronym letter in the accent color.
  • Latin only in eyebrows, as "Latin · English". Roman numerals for levels only; xp, counts and days in digits.
  • Only the theme's named colors, the mono font, and OWL's nodes: one Canvas for the map, Rows, Columns, Texts, a Progress, a Sparkline, an Input, Buttons and Popups around it.
  • From Forum: the nine buildings, lit windows for today, @ at the top quest, ? for a check-in, night by the clock, rain for overdue quests.
  • From Castra: every building stands on its insula block, which shows the level, the week (▪ today, ▫ earlier in 7 days) and the 7-day count; the route's stops are numbered on the roofs.
