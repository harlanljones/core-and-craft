# The CORE & CRAFT Personal Framework — Consolidated Version 4

## A dual-category operating system for career execution, life foundations, and long-term leverage

**C.O.R.E. — Foundations & Capital** protects career direction, emotional health, relationships, and financial security. **C.R.A.F.T. — Execution, Systems & Growth** develops mastery, recovery, tools, ethical grounding, and time management. Recovery and ethics are foundations too; the categories organize the work, not its importance.

This version restores the original personal commitments and selects relevant improvements from [iteration 1](CORE-CRAFT-framework-v1.md), [iteration 2](CORE-CRAFT-framework-v2.md), and [iteration 3](CORE-CRAFT-framework-v3.md). It is a consolidation, **not a fourth research audit**. Source IDs link to the [iteration 3 evidence audit](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md), which records the research and limitations; the [first](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v1.md) and [second](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v2.md) audits preserve earlier decisions.

**How to use it.** The 90-minute sprint, daily log, two weekly interactions, study block, and Sunday review are chosen operating standards—not proven universal doses. Protect them as defaults; reschedule or adapt for illness, emergencies, caregiving, safety, or obligations outside your control. Respond to urgent concerns immediately. The combined framework is not a validated intervention or a substitute for individualized professional advice.

# Part 1: C.O.R.E. — Foundations & Capital

## C — Career

**Definition.** Direct professional effort toward meaningful outcomes and sustainable advancement. Career capital includes skills, trusted relationships, visible contributions, and judgment; autonomy also depends on available authority and resources.

**Operating rule.** On workdays, protect a **90-minute focus sprint** for an agreed outcome when feasible. Keep a **daily milestone log**: record a real step, learning, decision, or blocker and its next action; do not invent a win. Record changed expectations and clarify scope or resources with the person who can decide. Preserve permitted evidence of contributions and credit collaborators. The sprint length is a personal default, not a proven biological optimum. ## O — Outlook & Emotional Health

**Definition.** Recognize emotions and stress, respond in context, recover, and seek support. Difficult feelings are not failures, and expression depends on safety.

**Operating rule.** At shutdown, list unresolved tasks and their next actions; adjust or stop if the practice increases rumination. In the Sunday audit, note significant stressors, responses, and what helped. Use PERMA-Profiler only for **general well-being**, not acute stress. If useful, PSS-10 reflects perceived stress over the **past month**, not a single event; weekly administrations overlap. Use original wording and scoring and check current permissions; neither instrument diagnoses a condition. Seek help when concerned without waiting for a score or review. [OH1](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#oh1) [OH2](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#oh2) [OH5](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#oh5) [OH6](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#oh6) [OH7](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#oh7)

## R — Relationships & Connections

**Definition.** Maintain safe, meaningful ties through belonging, shared experience, support, and boundaries. Connection depends on the quality and function of relationships as well as their frequency; chosen solitude is not the same as loneliness.

**Operating rule.** Aim for **at least two mutually welcome, non-transactional interactions each week**, favoring people and activities that are restorative. Care or a difficult conversation can still matter even when it is not immediately energizing. Respect safety and capacity; the two-interaction goal is a personal reminder, not a validated health threshold. [RC1](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#rc1) [RC2](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#rc2) [RC3](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#rc3) [RC5](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#rc5)

## E — Economic Security & Finances

**Definition.** Build capacity to meet obligations, absorb shocks, and make choices through understandable cash flow, manageable debt, accessible reserves, and suitable long-term plans.

**Operating rule.** Automate affordable baseline savings and, when appropriate, investments. Balance essential bills, an accessible reserve, high-interest debt, applicable benefits, fees, taxes, and risk; no single sequence fits everyone. Check balances, upcoming payments, alerts, and failed transfers during the weekly review. Contact creditors or qualified help early if a shortfall is likely. Automation reduces administration; it cannot guarantee security or create missing income. # Part 2: C.R.A.F.T. — Execution, Systems & Growth

## C — Competence & Mastery

**Definition.** Develop knowledge, technical skill, and judgment through deliberate study, practice, feedback, and application beyond the immediate ticket.

**Operating rule.** Protect a **dedicated weekly study block** away from ticket pressure. Study a relevant paper, model, or architecture question; reconstruct or apply the idea, compare the result and your confidence with reliable feedback, then try it in a different case. Work tasks can also teach, but they do not replace the protected study block by default. Transfer to professional judgment must be checked, not assumed. [CM1](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#cm1) [CM2](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#cm2) [CM5](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#cm5)

## R — Recovery & Physical Health

**Definition.** Protect sleep, tolerable movement, nourishment, and rest as foundations for health and a good life—not just productivity.

**Operating rule.** Keep a sleep window and movement appropriate to your health; do not routinely use sleep as a buffer for late-night work. Shut down development work, place devices away from bed where safe, and stage activity gear to reduce friction. Discuss persistent sleep problems with a clinician; for diagnosed chronic insomnia, ask about CBT-I. If physical or mental exertion causes delayed or disproportionate worsening, such as post-exertional malaise in ME/CFS, **do not push through or automatically increase activity**; seek individualized clinical advice. [RH1](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#rh1) [RH2](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#rh2) [RH3](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#rh3) [RH7](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#rh7)

## A — Agents & Systems

**Definition.** Use workflows, toolchains, scripts, AI assistance, and agents to reduce operational friction while preserving quality, privacy, security, and human accountability.

**Operating rule.** Delegate bounded, verifiable tasks; use a simpler script when sufficient. Limit functions and permissions in downstream systems, treat external content as untrusted data, validate generated output, and require approval outside the agent for consequential actions. Compare the workflow with its baseline—including setup, review, rework, failed attempts, cost, and quality—and keep, change, or stop it based on benefit **and** acceptable risk. [AS1](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#as1) [AS3](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#as3) [AS4](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#as4) [AS6](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#as6)

## F — Faith & Ethics

**Definition.** Ground decisions, long-term purpose, and daily integrity in the religious, moral, or philosophical principles you hold to be timeless. These commitments are normative; research does not prove or disprove a creed.

**Operating rule.** Apply those principles to career ambitions, technical output, and relationships. For consequential decisions, consider duties, foreseeable harms, affected people, and burdens of care; seek counsel, decline or escalate, and repair harm when appropriate and safe. Honor your own tradition without assuming that a checklist resolves every conflict. [FE1](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#fe1) [FE4](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#fe4) [FE7](CORE-CRAFT-evidence-audit-v3.md#fe7)

## T — Time Management & Planning

**Definition.** Coordinate focus, commitments, transitions, interruptions, and recovery around actual capacity and priorities.

**Operating rule.** Schedule demanding work during your subjectively sharp hours and protect **buffer zones** around focus blocks. Maintain a route for urgent interruptions and leave a next-action cue when switching tasks. Adjust the calendar for changed demands or fatigue without sacrificing sleep to chase a supposed biological peak. # Weekly C.R.A.F.T. & C.O.R.E. Pulse Check

Review on **Sunday**. Answer yes, no, or not applicable; note one useful adjustment. This is personal accountability, not a diagnostic instrument or a measure of worth. Handle urgent issues before the review.

## C.O.R.E. audit

- **[C] Career:** Did I make meaningful progress and record milestones, blockers, or changed expectations?
- **[O] Outlook & Emotional Health:** Did I notice stressors, respond constructively, and make room for recovery or support?
- **[R] Relationships & Connections:** Did I make room for two welcome, non-transactional interactions and appropriate boundaries?
- **[E] Economic Security & Finances:** Did I check upcoming obligations, balances, alerts, and the reserve/debt tradeoff?

## C.R.A.F.T. audit

- **[C] Competence & Mastery:** Did I protect study time, practice an idea, and identify feedback or a next application?
- **[R] Recovery & Physical Health:** Did I protect sleep, tolerable movement, nourishment, and my work shutdown?
- **[A] Agents & Systems:** Did automation reduce friction after review, rework, cost, quality, and risk were considered?
- **[F] Faith & Ethics:** Did important decisions reflect my principles, and is any clarification or repair needed?
- **[T] Time Management & Planning:** Did I protect focus and buffers while adapting to energy and obligations?

**Scope.** The research supports some components and informs others; it does not validate CORE & CRAFT as one package. For immediate danger, contact local emergency or crisis services. For health concerns, serious financial difficulty, or consequential decisions beyond your expertise, seek appropriate qualified support.
